写给每一个女孩，与爱她们的人

你们是否听过“女孩子应该活成什么样”？

或许你和大多数人一样，都曾被这句话束缚过。但没关系，我们都是在这些条条框框里长大，又一点点把它们拆掉的人。

于是，有两位可爱的女孩提出一个想法：能不能造一个名为“予我之境”的世界，让每个女孩都看见——你可以温柔，也可以有锋；你可以松弛，也可以有主见。你的领地，永远由你做主。于是我们决定创作这本书。

我们同样还在学习，也希望这本小书，能够给一些人带去改变。

For every girl, and those who love them. Have you ever been told what a girl should be? Perhaps, like many of us, you once felt confined by those words. We grew up inside these rules and learned to take them apart, piece by piece. Two wonderful girls imagined a realm where every girl can be gentle yet bold, relaxed yet certain. Your world is always yours to lead. So we made this book. We are still learning too, and hope this little book can bring change to someone.

坚守立场

STAND YOUR GROUND

为自己发声

Speak Up for Yourself

勇敢捍卫你的边界。学会温和却坚定地表达想法，认清自身价值，永远不要为妥协交出自我。

Defend your boundaries with courage. Express yourself gently but firmly, know your worth, and never surrender who you are for compromise.

温柔生长

GENTLE GROWTH

守护属于自己的安宁

Protect Your Own Peace

照料你的内心世界，在心底培育从容与柔软，搭建专属于你的宁静小天地。

Tend to your inner world, nurture calm and softness within, and create a tranquil little realm of your own.

自启远航

SET SAIL

循着自己的星光起航

Sail by Your Own Starlight

你的航线不由他人书写。手握望远镜，掌舵前行，以自己的方式去探索广阔世界。

Set sail by your own light. Chart your course, and explore the wider world in your own way.

执掌生活

OWN YOUR LIFE

把控自己的人生

Take Charge of Your Life

认真对待生活里每一件小事，掌控自己的事务，按属于你的节奏稳步前行。

Take every small matter seriously, manage your own affairs, and move forward steadily at your own pace.

掌控当下

OWN THE MOMENT

身在何处，主场即在何处

Wherever You Are, Make It Yours

你所抵达之处，便是你的归属。坦然拥抱欢愉，你就是每一个场景的主人。

Wherever you arrive can become a place of belonging. Embrace joy openly—you are the one who owns every scene.

清醒洞察

CLEAR-EYED

看透，却不戳破

See, Yet Stay Silent

以锐利眼光观察世间，你可以看透诸多真相，但表达的选择权永远握在自己手中。

Observe the world with sharp insight. You may see through many truths, yet you get to choose when to speak.

坚守本心

STAND FAST

守护你的边界

Shield Your Boundary

坚守内心的信念，你拥有守护自我领地的力量，不必向外界的杂音妥协。

Hold firm to your inner faith. You have the strength to guard your territory without yielding to outside noise.

光影入场

ENTER THE STORY

让想象在银幕前发生

Let Imagination Take the Screen

在明暗交错的光影里，为自己留一席位置。故事尚未开始，而你已经身在其中。

Take your seat where light and shadow meet. The story has not begun, yet you are already part of it.
